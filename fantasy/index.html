Production boundary

How connections are handled

  • The browser connector reuses an ESPN session without reading its cookies.
  • Your ESPN password, verification codes, and session values stay on ESPN.
  • Projection and news feeds enter through one versioned, normalized endpoint.
  • RotoWire data is accepted only when the endpoint marks it as licensed.
  • Every provider record carries an update time, confidence, and provenance.

Imported league data stays on your device. The connector returns ESPN league data directly to this page; licensed evidence still uses the normalized source endpoint.
