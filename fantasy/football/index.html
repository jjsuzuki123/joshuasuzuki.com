            How values work

            Player value

            ~45% projected points under your scoring (with positional VORP), 25% market/ADP-style anchor, 20% skill/efficiency fixtures, plus bounded role/injury adjustments. Superflex lifts QBs; TEP lifts TEs.

            Roster slots

            Unequal trades are not scored as “more players = better.” Receiving more players pays a roster-space tax near replacement value. Consolidating into fewer players earns a premium — starting spots are scarce.
