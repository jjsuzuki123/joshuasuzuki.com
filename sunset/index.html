How the score works

This is a deterministic weather model, not a claim that beauty can be measured perfectly. It samples the hourly forecast at the exact local sunset, then weighs the atmospheric conditions that tend to produce visible color.

Afterglow quality index 0 to 100
  1. Mid + high cloud canvas32%
  2. Low-cloud horizon opening28%
  3. Visibility14%
  4. Dry sunset window11%
  5. Humidity8%
  6. Aerosol balance7%
01

Clouds need height

Mid and high clouds can stay sunlit after the ground falls into shadow. The model favors partial coverage and penalizes both empty and fully overcast skies.

02

The horizon needs a gap

Low cloud and fog can cut off direct light. Their score also uses the hour-before trend, so a clearing horizon gets a small lift and a closing one gets a penalty.

03

Hard blockers still win

Thunderstorms, dense fog, very low visibility, or a near-solid low cloud deck cap the final score. Missing aerosol data is removed and the remaining weights rebalance.
