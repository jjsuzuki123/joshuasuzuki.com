How much does a company spend on AI coding tools?

Enter a company domain. Spendscope resolves its public GitHub footprint, collects adoption evidence for Claude Code, Cursor, OpenAI Codex, GitHub Copilot, and Devin, stores the readings, and models a monthly spend range.

First scan of a domain queues an enrichment pass (~30–60s). Readings are cached, so repeat lookups are instant.

How the estimate is modeled

1 · Resolve

The domain is matched to its public GitHub organization(s) by verified website and email, then sized by public repositories and members.

2 · Enrich

A worker collects deterministic readings: CLAUDE.md and .cursor rules files, commits co-authored by Claude or Cursor agents, pull requests opened by Devin, Copilot, and Codex bots, plus cited web mentions. Every reading is stored with its source.

3 · Score

ACES v1 turns readings into a per-vendor adoption score with a saturating noisy-OR, estimates seats from developer headcount, and prices them with blended list prices into a low / mid / high range.

Estimates are modeled from public signals — never billing data. Private repositories are invisible, so real usage is usually higher than what public evidence shows.
